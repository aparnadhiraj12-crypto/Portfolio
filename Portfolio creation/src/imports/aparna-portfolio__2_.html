About Aparna

More than the résumé

Aparna is a Software Engineering student at Jain University (2024–2028) who moves fluidly between frontend craft, backend systems, and applied AI research. She is currently a Machine Learning Intern at CSIR – Indian Institute of Chemical Technology, contributing to ML-driven research and data-led problem solving.

Her work has already produced two published papers — on hallucinations in large language models, and on IoT-and-AI-driven precision agriculture — alongside two granted patents for a civic complaint-routing system and an agricultural yield-optimisation system. She has been shortlisted at Smart India Hackathon (national and college level) and reached the semi-finals of Bajaj Finance's HackRx 6.0 and the One Earth International Hackathon.

Outside of shipping code, she has led sprint planning as a Project Management intern, shaped frontend experience for a government complaint portal, and grown communities as a Campus Ambassador — proof that she is as comfortable coordinating people as she is debugging a model.
